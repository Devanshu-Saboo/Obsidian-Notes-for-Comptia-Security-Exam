Corrective control types are implemented when you wish to apply a control after an event has been detected. Sometimes for continuing a business after a particular downtime. 

This is used commonly in real production systems, when an attack has taken place but you cannot shut down the entire system to focus on the attack, instead a corrective control type security would help keeping business operational while the attacker is isolated on the system and removed.
