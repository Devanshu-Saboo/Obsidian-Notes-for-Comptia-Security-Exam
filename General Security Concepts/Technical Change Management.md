In the previous section we talked about changes from the client side, lets take a look at it from the IT department side who actually has to implement these changes.

Firstly on the technical side we have allow lists and deny lists, these are simply a list of applications that you may or may not install. These decisions are taken on various factors such as versions of the software as some versions are vulnerable and can be easily attacked by an threat actor. 

There are two ways to use the allow and deny list, which are simply to the allow list direction or the deny list direction. In the allow list direction, only software that have been approved by the IT team may be installed whereas in the deny list direction, every software can be installed and used other than the software that the IT team has blacklisted by adding to the deny list.
