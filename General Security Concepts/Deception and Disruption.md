In Information Security, there are a number of attackers that will attempt to attack your organization. Up to now most of what we have looked at has been about keeping attackers out, but there is another approach, which is to let them in on purpose, and into something fake.

To better defend against attackers, to understand how they work and how they attack systems, and to trap them and try to get any traceable information from an attacker, we have created these concepts, which all begin with "honey":
1. [[HoneyPot]]: A fake system that looks like the real thing
2. [[HoneyNet]]: A whole fake network of honeypots
3. [[HoneyFile]]: A fake file that looks valuable
4. [[HoneyToken]]: Fake data that can be traced if it leaks

The big advantage is that legitimate users should never be touching any of these, so any activity on them is automatically suspicious, and at the same time we get to study the attacker without risking real data.
