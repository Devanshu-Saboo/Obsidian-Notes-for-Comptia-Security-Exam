Directive control types are implemented when you wish to use weaker security controls, wherein you direct people to take an extra security step but it is not enforced in the slightest.

A good example of this would be a sign stating authorized personal only. It states that only authorized people are allowed, but without any security guards or any other physical controls to actually prevent, anyone may enter.
