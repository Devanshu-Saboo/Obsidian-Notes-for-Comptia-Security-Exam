Availability is simply ensuring that authorized users are able to access the data that they wish to access at any and all times. This also includes making the process as fast as possible for authorized users while still denying access to unauthorized users for data that they do not have access to.

Data that is perfectly secret and perfectly unchanged is still useless if the people who need it cannot get to it. ==🟢ex. An online store that is down during a sale, A hospital system that is unreachable when a doctor needs a patient's records==

Some of the common ways to protect availability are:
1. ==🟠Redundancy==: Having a duplicate system ready to take over, like the primary and secondary systems in [[Technical Change Management Downtime]]
2. ==🟠Backups==: So data can be restored if it is lost or corrupted
3. ==🟠Power backups==: UPS and generators so a power cut does not take the systems down

The attacks that target this objective the most are Denial of Service (DoS) attacks, where an attacker floods a system with so much traffic that real users cannot get in.
