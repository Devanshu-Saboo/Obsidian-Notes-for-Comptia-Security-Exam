Honeynets are, in simple terms, multiple honeypots with fake information that pretend to be the real server so as to trick attackers better. With the evolution of attackers and their tools, attackers are able to better discern between a [[HoneyPot]] and a real server. To better deceive attackers and understand their motives and techniques, we use honeynets, which make it that much harder for a threat actor to discern if a system is fake or real.

Where a honeypot is a single fake machine, a honeynet is a whole fake network, complete with fake servers, fake devices and fake traffic. The attacker can move around between them like they would on a real network, which means we can watch what they do at every step, not just the first.
