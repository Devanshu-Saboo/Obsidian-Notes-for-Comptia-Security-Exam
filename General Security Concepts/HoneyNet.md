Honeynet's are in simple terms multiple honeypots with fake information that pretend to be the real server to as to trick attackers better. With the evolution of attackers and information, attackers are able to better discern between a honeypot device and a real server. To better deceive and understand the attackers motives and techniques, we use honeynets that make it that much more harder for a threat actor to discern if a system is fake or real.
