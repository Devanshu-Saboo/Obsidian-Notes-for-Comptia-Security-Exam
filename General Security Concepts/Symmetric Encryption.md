Encryption is a process which is used to ensure confidentiality. In simple terms if I talk to my friend and I wanted to ensure our conversation is not understood by anyone else even if they overhear us, I would use encryption.

Symmetric encryption is a type of encryption that uses keys to encrypt and decrypt data. In symmetric encryption, the same key is used for both encryption and decryption so both me and my friend would use a key say "21" as a key to encrypt and decrypt our conversation.

This process is very fast to implement but for multiple people the keys required would be exponential making it difficult to keep and maintain keys which is why, we also have asymmetric encryption.
