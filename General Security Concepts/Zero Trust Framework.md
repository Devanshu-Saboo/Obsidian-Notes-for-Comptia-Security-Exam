Zero Trust is a framework that simply means that each device must authenticate itself in some form whenever performing an action inside the internal network. Its motto is basically "never trust, always verify".

In the older way of doing security, we trusted anyone who got inside the network, kind of like a castle with a moat where everyone inside the walls is safe. The problem is that once an attacker got past the firewall, they could move around freely. With zero trust, being inside the network does not mean anything, every device and every request has to prove itself each time.

There are 2 major planes in this framework namely:
1. [[Data Plane]]
2. [[Control Plane]]
