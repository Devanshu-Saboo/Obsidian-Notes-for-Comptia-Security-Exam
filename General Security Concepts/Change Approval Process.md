Most companies already have a change approval process in place and use it constantly to make changes in the organization. 

This is a formal process for making change because it can help avoid downtime and mistakes ensuring the network is unaffected and business may operate at full capacity even once the changes are implemented.

There are various steps to this process such as:
1. Completing the request forms
2. Determining the purpose of the change
3. Identifying the scope of the change
4. Scheduling the date and time for the change
5. Determining affected systems and their impact
6. Analyzing the risk associated with the change
7. Getting approval from the change control board
8. Getting end-user acceptance that the system is working fine on their end.
