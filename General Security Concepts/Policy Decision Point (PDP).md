This is the software that will examine the data previously provided by the PEP and then finally take a decision on whether to grant, deny or revoke access to the device for the requested resources and further relaying this information back to PEP. 

PDP is further split into 2 sperate sections:
1. Policy Engine: This is responsible for the decision making inside the PDP based on the data collected by PEP.
2. Policy Administrator: This is responsible for the relaying the decision granted by the Policy Engine back to the PEP. This will also forward data such as any credentials that were created for the device through the PEP to the device.
