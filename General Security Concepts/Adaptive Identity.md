This is an addition on the earlier mentioned zero trust architecture, in zero trust we authenticate the device at every step even after they pass through the firewall connecting the internet and the organization network. 

In Adaptive Identity, we collect and consider even more information such as:==🟢 physical location of the device, IP address of the device, the type of connection etc.==

Depending on the data collected from above mentioned resources, if the system still flags the user as a risk it may attempt to authenticate the user with even more tests such as an OTP (One Time Password) using an authenticator app such as Microsoft or Google.
