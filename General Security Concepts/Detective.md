Detective control types are implemented when you wish to identify and/or log an intrusion attempt. This is not a guaranteed measure to prevent access and is used for the sole purpose of identifying intruders.

A detective control tells us that something happened (or is happening), which is what lets us respond to it. Without it, an attacker could be inside the network for months without anyone knowing. ==🟢ex. Log files, CCTV recordings, Motion detectors and alarms, Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS), Security audits==

It is also very useful *after* an incident since the logs and recordings help us understand how the attacker got in and what they touched. This is closely related to the idea of [[Accounting]] that we will look at in the AAA framework, since that is also about keeping a record of what happened. The sensors we mention in [[Physical Security]] are also detective controls.

Remember that this type only detects, it does not stop anything. Once a detective control raises the alarm, it is the [[Corrective]] controls that come in to fix it.
