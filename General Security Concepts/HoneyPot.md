A Honeypot is a fake system, server, firewall, switch etc. that contains fake information about the organization and its client details. The honeypot is created to trap an attacker into thinking they gained access to a real machine, which will help us study what sort of tactics they employ so we may then better secure our systems against similar threats.

It is usually set up so it looks like an easy and attractive target, for example a server with a weak login. Since no real employee has any reason to connect to it, any traffic it receives is automatically suspicious. Everything the attacker does on it is logged, which gives us an idea of their tools and methods.

It is important that the honeypot is kept isolated from the real network, since we do not want an attacker to use it as a stepping stone to the real systems.
