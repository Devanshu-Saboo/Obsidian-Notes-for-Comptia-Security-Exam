Deterrent control types are implemented when you wish to discourage an intrusion attempt. Basically make an attacker think twice before attacking. This is not a guaranteed measure to stop an attacker from accessing an asset, resource or location.

A deterrent works on the attacker's mind, they would have to decide that the effort or the risk of being caught is not worth it. ==🟢ex. Warning signs, Visible CCTV cameras, A security guard standing at the entrance, Good lighting around the building, A login banner warning that unauthorized access will be prosecuted==

The important thing is that nothing is physically stopping the attacker. A fake security camera on a wall can still work as a deterrent even though it records nothing, but it is certainly not a preventive control. This is also why a determined attacker will usually not be put off by a deterrent.

> [!joke] A "Smile, you're on camera" sign is the cheapest security guard you can buy.
