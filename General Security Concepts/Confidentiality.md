Confidentiality is simply ensuring that only authorized personnel have access to sensitive data. This includes preventing unauthorized people from accessing information that they should not have access to.

A good real life example is a hospital. A patient's medical records should only be visible to the doctors and nurses treating them, and not to every employee or random visitor. ==🟢ex. Medical records, Customer credit card details, Company trade secrets==

Some of the common ways we maintain confidentiality are:
1. ==🟠Encryption==: Makes the data unreadable without the key, see [[Encrypting Data]]
2. ==🟠Access controls==: Making sure each person only gets access to what they need, see [[Authorization]]
3. ==🟠Authentication==: Making sure the person is who they say they are before letting them in, see [[Authentication]]

Basically if an unauthorized person is able to read the data, even without changing anything, confidentiality has been broken.
