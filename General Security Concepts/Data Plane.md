This plane is responsible for the actual execution of actions that block and inhibit an attacker's movement inside the network ==🟢ex. Firewalls, Switches, Encrypting etc.==

It is the part that does the real work on the traffic, it processes the frames and packets, encrypts and decrypts data and follows the rules which have been set for it. ==🟠Think of it as the security staff at an airport who actually check each passenger.==

The data plane does not decide the rules on its own, it simply follows them. The rules come from the [[Control Plane]].
