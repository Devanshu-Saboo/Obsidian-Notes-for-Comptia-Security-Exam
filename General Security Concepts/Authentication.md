Prove you are who you say you are ==🟢ex. Username and Password==

This is the very first step in [[AAA Framework]], and nothing else can happen until it is done, since we cannot decide what you can access if we do not know who you are. The system checks the proof you give against what it already has on record.

It is not only people that have to authenticate, devices and systems do as well. A company authentication server may also have a firewall which will check for your Digital Signature or even a certificate provided by your Certificate Authority, which is how a system proves its identity without a password. We cover how those certificates work in [[Public Key Infrastructure]].

Later on in the course we will see that a password alone is not enough, so systems also ask for extra proof such as an OTP, which we already touched on in [[Adaptive Identity]].
