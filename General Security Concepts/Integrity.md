Integrity is simply ensuring that data that is unmodified by any unauthorized user. This may include ensuring communication between 2 parties are unmodified and that the original text sent between the two is untampered with. This can be done by hashing and comparing hashes of the messages. Digital signatures using asymmetric encryption on hashes can also be used to verify the contents of the data.
