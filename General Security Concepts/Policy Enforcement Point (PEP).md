This point in reality is a series of devices that collect all the information discussed so far (see [[Adaptive Identity]]) from devices that are trying to connect to any resource, trusted or untrusted.

I like to think of the PEP as the bouncer at the door of a club. The bouncer does not decide on their own who gets in, instead they check the person, pass the details on to the manager and then follow the manager's decision. In our case the manager is the [[Policy Decision Point (PDP)]].

After collecting all this data, the Policy Enforcement Point will transfer it to the Policy Decision Point. Once the decision comes back, the PEP is what actually enforces it by allowing, denying or revoking the connection to the resource.
