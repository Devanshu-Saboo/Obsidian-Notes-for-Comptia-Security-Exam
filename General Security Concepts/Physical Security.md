Physical security is everything that stops an attacker from simply walking up to our systems. Here is everything that is necessary for the exam, and these tie directly into the [[Physical Controls]] we looked at earlier.

### Barricades
These are physical obstructions such as ==🟠barricades== or bollards that limit access to places to only people and not vehicles or carts by leaving small gaps in between the barricades. This can also be a checkpoint guarded by a security guard and a movable barricade.

### Access Control Vestibule
There are also usually access points that are blocked off by doors unless you present some form of identification, and then every consecutive door would be further blocked until the first door has been closed. This is called an ==🟠access control vestibule== and it prevents people from sneaking in behind someone else.

### Video Surveillance
Video Surveillance or a CCTV camera alongside a motion detector would also help quickly identify any unauthorized access in a restricted zone. Even without motion sensors, you would still have the option of setting up a control room connected to multiple cameras and a guard to watch over the cameras for any suspicious activity.

### Security Guards and Badges
Furthermore, security guards can also be posted at key points of access to check and confirm employee identities, which can be easily proven by providing employees with access badges with their names and faces, ensuring no unauthorized person may enter.

### Lighting
One unconventional way to prevent attackers from infiltrating the premises would be to add lighting to all dark areas around the organization. This would help more easily identify and detect an attacker with the help of CCTVs or security guards on patrol.

### Sensors
The last physical controls necessary for the exam are sensors:
1. ==🟠Infrared==: Detects infrared radiation (heat), so it works in both light and dark areas. Has a slightly smaller range for detection than Microwave.
2. ==🟠Pressure==: Detects a change in force, so perhaps on tiles of secure rooms.
3. ==🟠Microwave==: Can be used to detect movement over a large area.
4. ==🟠Ultrasonic==: The most advanced sensor, which sends out ultrasonic waves and receives the reflected sound waves to detect motion or collisions.
