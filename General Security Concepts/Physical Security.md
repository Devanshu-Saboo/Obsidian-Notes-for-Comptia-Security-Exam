These are physical obstructions such as barricades that limit the access of places to only people and not carts by leaving small gaps in between the barricades. This can also be a checkpoint guarded by a security guard and a movable barricade. 

There are also usually access points that are blocked off by doors unless you present some form of identification and then every consequent door would then be further blocked until the first door has been closed.

Video Surveillance or a CCTV camera alongside a motion detector would also help quickly identify any unauthorized access in a restricted zone. Even without motion sensors, you would still have the option of setting up a control room connected to multiple cameras and a guard to watch over the cameras for any suspicious activity.

Furthermore Security guards can also be posted at key point of access to check and confirm employee identities which can be easily proven by providing employees with access badges with their names and faces ensuring no unauthorized person may enter.

One unconventional way to prevent attackers from infiltrating the premises would be to add lighting to all dark areas around the organization. This would help more easily identify and detect an attacker with the help of CCTV's or security guards on patrol.

The last physical controls necessary for the exam are sensors:
1. Infrared: Which detects radiation in both light and dark areas. Has a slightly smaller range for detection than Microwave.
2. Pressure: Which detects a change in force, so perhaps on tiles of secure rooms.
3. Microwave: Which can be used to detect movement over a large area.
4. Ultrasonic: The most advanced sensor which sends out ultrasonic waves which receive reflected sound waves to detect motion or collisions.
