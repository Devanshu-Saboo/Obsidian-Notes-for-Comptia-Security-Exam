Preventive control types are implemented when you wish to block access to any particular resource, asset or location. This type of control is typically used when you want to "guarantee no unauthorized access" to a particular resource, asset or location.

The key thing about a preventive control is that it works *before* the attack happens and physically or logically stops it. ==🟢ex. Firewall rules, Locked doors, A security guard stopping people at the entrance, Disabling an account that is no longer used, Operating System hardening==

A good way to separate this from the next type, [[Deterrent]], is that a deterrent only convinces an attacker not to try, whereas a preventive control actually stops them if they do try. A "beware of dog" sign is a deterrent, a locked gate is preventive.

In real life no control is a 100% guarantee, but when we say preventive we mean this is the control that is *meant* to stop access outright, instead of just recording it or discouraging it.
