Authentication, Authorization and Accounting. These 3 steps happen in order every time someone accesses a system, and together they answer 3 questions: who are you, what are you allowed to do and what did you do.
1. [[Authentication]]: Who are you?
2. [[Authorization]]: What are you allowed to access?
3. [[Accounting]]: What did you actually do?

A simple example is going into an office building. You scan your badge at the door (authentication), the badge lets you into your own floor but not the server room (authorization) and the system logs the time that you entered (accounting).

In larger organizations a dedicated AAA server usually handles this for every device and user on the network, so that these decisions are made in one central place and not on each individual system.
