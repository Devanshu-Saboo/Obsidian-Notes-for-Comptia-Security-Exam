This plane is responsible for the controls that we set into place and for managing the [[Data Plane]] ==🟢ex. Rules, Policies, Firewall rules, Routing tables==

If we continue the airport example from the data plane, this is the management that decides *what* the rules are, such as who is allowed to board and what items are banned, and then tells the security staff to follow them. The control plane defines and configures the rules and the data plane carries them out.

This is also where the policy decisions made in the later parts of zero trust, such as the [[Policy Decision Point (PDP)]], end up being applied.
