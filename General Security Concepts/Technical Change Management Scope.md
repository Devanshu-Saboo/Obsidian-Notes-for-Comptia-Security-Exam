As mentioned previously, the scope of a change is very important to consider as small routine updates can possibly cause global havoc. That is why the scope is always well researched and provided to the change control team, which will either grant or deny the request.

The scope is simply the boundaries of the change, what is being changed and what is not. ==🟢ex. "Update the firewall rules for the HR network" is a scope, it does not include touching any other firewall or any other network.==

In the case of accepting the change and obtaining, say, a 5 hour window to make the necessary changes, the scope of the change will always be limited to the scope mentioned previously and no other changes can be made in any extra time that you may or may not obtain. This is to avoid anyone making extra "quick fixes" that have not been reviewed.

Sometimes, if a case is taking longer than expected or if the scope has to be increased for a change to take effect and is expected to take more time, additional time can be granted. This depends on a case by case scenario and will have to be approved by the change control board, the same group we saw in the [[Change Approval Process]].
