As mentioned previously the scope of a change is very important to consider as small routine updates can possibly cause global havoc. That is why the scope is always well researched and provided to the change control team which will either grant or deny the request. 

In the case of accepting the change and obtaining a 5 hour window to make the necessary changes, the scope of the change will always be limited to the scope mentioned previously and no other changes can be made in any extra time that you may or may not obtain.

Sometimes if a case is taking longer than expected or if the scope has to be increased for a change to take affect which is expected to take more time, additional time can be granted for changes to take place but this depends on a case by case scenario and will have to be approved by the change control board.
