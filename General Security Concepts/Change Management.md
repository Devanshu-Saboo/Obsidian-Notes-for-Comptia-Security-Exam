Simply put, whenever you want to upgrade/update software, make an OS change, install a new application, do a version update etc. you have to put in a request to IT for this process, which may take time to implement.

The reason behind all this is that a change, even a tiny one, can break things. A change can affect other systems that rely on the one being changed, cause downtime or even create new security holes. Having a formal process makes sure that each change is thought through, approved, tested and can be undone if it goes wrong.

We will look at this from two sides:
1. [[Change Approval Process]]: The business side, how a change gets requested and approved
2. [[Technical Change Management]]: The IT side, how the change is actually carried out
