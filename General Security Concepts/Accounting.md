What resources did you use when you were logged into the account ==🟢ex. Login Time, Data sent etc.==

This is the last step of the [[AAA Framework]] and is simply keeping a record of everything the user did after they were authenticated and authorized. Some more of the things that are tracked are ==🟠how long the user was logged in, what data was sent and received and which resources they accessed==.

This is useful for a few reasons:
1. ==🟠Auditing==: Checking that people only did what they were allowed to do
2. ==🟠Investigating incidents==: Working out what happened after a breach, which is why it works like a [[Detective]] control
3. ==🟠Billing==: Charging for usage, for example for how much data was used
